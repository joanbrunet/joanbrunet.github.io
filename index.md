# About

  I am a PhD researcher at the Universitat de Barcelona working on the intersection between nationalism studies, political theory and political economy, under the supervision of Daniel Cetrà.  
  
  My academic background includes a [Master's degree in Governance](https://www.upf.edu/web/master-governance) from Pompeu Fabra University in Barcelona, [postgraduate studies in data analysis for political analysis and public management](https://www.ub.edu/analista-de-dades/) from Barcelona University and a [bachelor's degree in Political Science and Public Administration](https://www.uab.cat/web/estudiar/ehea-degrees/general-information-1216708259085.html?param1=1228291015798) from the Autonomous University of Barcelona.

I currently work at Universitat de Barcelona as a research assistant in the project Paths to (De)polarisation in Europe: Comparing Political Discourses on Immigration in Catalonia, Bavaria, Flanders, and Scotland; funded by the Institut d'Estudis de l'Autogovern. Most recently, I worked as a researcher at the [Center for Leadership and Sustainability at ESADE](https://www.esade.edu/faculty-research/es/catedra-liderazgos-y-sostenibilidad) in Barcelona (2023-2025), where I analyzed corporate sustainability practices across 70+ Spanish listed firms, combining longitudinal data analysis with interviews with C-level sustainability executives. Previously, in 2023, I held a research assistant position at the [Climate and Environment group in the Johns Hopkins University - Pompeu Fabra University (JHU-UPF) Public Policy Center](https://www.upf.edu/web/jhu-ppc/climate), working on environmental policy and data management. 


On a personal level, I am interested in electronic and ambient music.
